Methodology

4/4 alignment: price > SMA10w, SMA20w, SMA43w (10-month), SMA87w (20-month) — identical to live screener.

Realized vol: SPY 13-week rolling std of weekly returns × √52 (annualized). Computed from same 5-year price history.

Vol regimes: Low <15% (calm/trending), Medium 15-25% (normal), High >25% (elevated/crisis).

Non-4/4 baseline: weeks when A+ tickers are NOT 4/4 aligned, sampled every 13w to reduce autocorrelation. Tests whether MA timing adds value beyond quality alone.

Excluded (insufficient history): CRCL, FIG, SLDE, X

Educational framework validation only. Not financial advice. Past backtest results do not predict future performance.
